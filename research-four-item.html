Regionalized Responses: The Role of Regional Integration Organizations in Transboundary Emergency Management

Postdoctoral research
(2026-2028)

This postdoctoral research project seeks to advance the understanding of the role played by Regional Integration Organizations (RIOs) in managing transboundary emergencies, using the COVID-19 pandemic as a paradigmatic case. Through a comparative analysis of sixteen RIOs, the project will systematically examine three core dimensions: (i) the nature and scope of regional emergency measures adopted during the pandemic, (ii) the institutional and contextual factors shaping the variation in regional responses, and (iii) the measurable impact of selected emergency interventions on member states. The project employs multi-methods design, incorporating computational text analysis, multivariate panel data techniques, and the Synthetic Control Method (SCM). By integrating theoretical frameworks from international organization studies and crisis management scholarship, this research contributes to the broader debate on regional governance, institutional resilience, and multilevel crisis coordination. Its findings will provide actionable insights into how regional institutions can be better designed and empowered to respond to future transboundary challenges. It will be conducted under the academic supervision of Prof. Dr. Diana Panke (Freie Universität Berlin) and Prof. Dr. Arjen Boin (Leiden University).
KEYWORDS: Regional Integration Organizations, Transboundary Emergencies, Crisis Management, COVID-19, Emergency Measures Index, Regional Governance.
